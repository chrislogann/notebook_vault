# 03 Mythology

## Overview
> The study and categorization of mythology, specifically focusing on how myths serve as active, weaponized instruments of statecraft, psychological warfare, and military mobilization across various historical contexts.

## Core Concepts
- Ruling regimes and revolutionary vanguards systematically construct and deploy mythic narratives to provide spiritual justification for violence.
- Engineered myths are utilized to sanitize historical defeats and manipulate the loyalties of both domestic populations and foreign observers.
