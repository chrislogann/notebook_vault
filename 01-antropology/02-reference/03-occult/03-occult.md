# 03 Occult

## Overview
> The anthropological study of hidden, esoteric, or secret knowledge and practices, often examined as foundational elements of fringe belief systems and cultural sub-structures.

## Core Concepts
- **Esoteric Traditions:** Practices and knowledge systems reserved for initiates or a select few within a broader societal context.
- **Cultural Function:** How occult beliefs serve as alternative power structures, social rebellion, or mechanisms for understanding the unknown within human societies.
- **Intersection with Mythology:** Occult practices frequently draw upon, reinterpret, or integrate established mythologies to legitimize their rituals and esoteric beliefs.
