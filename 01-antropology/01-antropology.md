# 03 Anthropology

## Overview
> The macro domain encompassing the study of human societies, cultures, their development, and the foundational belief systems that shape human behavior and societal structures.

## Core Concepts
- **Cultural and Societal Evolution:** The examination of how human societies organize, develop, and interact across different historical and geographical contexts.
- **Belief Systems and Ideology:** The study of how foundational narratives, traditions, and esoteric practices function within a society. 
- **Instrumental Narratives:** Analyzing how concepts like myths and the occult are not merely passive traditions, but active elements used to influence culture, politics, and power dynamics.
