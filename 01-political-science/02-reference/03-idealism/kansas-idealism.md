# Kansas Idealism

## Overview
> The foundational statecraft of early Kansas was driven by grassroots idealism, emphasizing government free of elite ambition and an uncompromising defense of universal human rights.

## Core Concepts
- **Statecraft Free of Personal Ambition:** Voters deliberately populated the Wyandotte Constitutional Convention with inexperienced "common farmers" and middle-class delegates, acting on the idealistic expectation that these men had no personal ambition to gratify or friends to favor.
- **Uncompromising Defense of Human Rights:** During convention debates, delegates like S.D. Houston provided highly principled defenses of human liberties and female suffrage, declaring it historically impossible for any power to permanently curtail human rights.
