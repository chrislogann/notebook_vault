# Anatomy Of Idealism

## Overview
> A structural framework categorizing the psychological origins of political idealism and its predictable lifecycle, from the weaponization of high-minded beliefs to its inevitable collision with raw governance.

## Core Concepts
- **Typology of Historical Idealism:** Idealism manifests in four primary strains: Naive/Sentimental (assuming frictionless transition), Romantic/Escapist (sublimating privileged identity), Doctrinal/Ruthless (rejecting emotion for deterministic logic), and Ethical/Principled (uncompromising defense of rights).
- **Abstraction and Weaponization:** Movements depersonalize enemies to maintain the efficiency of violence, allowing the most principled adherents to commit atrocities by viewing victims through the lens of a "social system" or historical necessity.
- **Collision with Raw Reality:** Idealistic movements suffer an "Euphoria Gap," inevitably fracturing when the intoxicating rhetoric of spontaneous meetings meets environmental hardships, starvation, and the cold mechanics of state power.
- **The Heroic Individual:** In the face of systemic decay and abstracted violence, grounded individuals (like Gabor Földes protecting his oppressors) provide an enduring moral counterweight that outlasts sweeping utopian movements.
