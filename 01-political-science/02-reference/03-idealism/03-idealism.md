# 03 Idealism

## Overview
> Idealism serves as both the architect of revolution and the psychological fuel for statecraft, originating from a complex spectrum of psychological needs and social aspirations.

## Core Concepts
- **Typology of Idealism:** Historical idealism falls into distinct categories: naive/sentimental, romantic/escapist, doctrinal/ruthless, and ethical/principled, each with its own structural vulnerabilities.
- **Weaponization of Ideals:** In civil conflict, high-minded beliefs are abstracted and weaponized to depersonalize the enemy and bridge the gap between human morality and the harsh duties of war.
- **Collision with Reality:** Utopian movements possess an inherent fragility, often fracturing or collapsing when the "joy of the present" transitions into the brutal mechanics of governance and state survival.
- **The Moral Counterweight:** When systems decay into ruthlessness, grounded, ethical individuals often emerge as vital moral counterweights against abstracted state violence.
