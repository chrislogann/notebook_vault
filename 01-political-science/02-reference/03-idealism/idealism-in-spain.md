# Idealism In Spain

## Overview
> During the Spanish Civil War, idealism manifested across a broad spectrum, ranging from the naive optimism of anarchists and the romantic guilt of foreign intellectuals to the deterministic ruthlessness of party militants.

## Core Concepts
- **Romantic and Escapist Idealism:** Middle-class Anglo-Saxon intellectuals volunteered for the Republic to sublimate a privileged identity and escape domestic complacency, tapping into a "Byronic tradition" of foreign military ventures.
- **Naive Anarchist Optimism:** Early Spanish anarchism functioned as a secular religion where converts lived ascetic lives, believing that a single uprising would instantly unlock the natural human state of mutual aid and spontaneous creativity.
- **Doctrinal Ruthlessness:** Young communists embraced "scientific socialism," utilizing a deterministic belief and party discipline to justify a ruthless rejection of sentimentality in the pursuit of a better society.
- **Abstraction of Violence:** Both extremes weaponized high-minded ideology to depersonalize the conflict, from Nationalist youth instructed to "Kill without hate!" to CNT leaders claiming their bullets killed a "social system" rather than individual men.
