# Hungarian Idealism

## Overview
> The 1956 Hungarian Revolution was characterized by early euphoric idealism and spontaneous mass organization, contrasted by the grounded moral courage of individuals resisting mob violence.

## Core Concepts
- **Euphoric "People Power":** The revolution's dawn was defined by breathless excitement and intoxicating rhetoric of liberty, resulting in endlessly debated resolutions that grew increasingly unrealistic as citizens became drunk with the present joy.
- **Individual Moral Courage:** Ethical idealism was epitomized by figures like theater director Gabor Földes, who demonstrated extreme moral courage by risking his own life to protect captured secret police (AVO) officers from being lynched by a vengeful crowd in Mosonmagyaróvár.
