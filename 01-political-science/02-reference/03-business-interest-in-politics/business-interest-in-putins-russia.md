# Business Interest In Putins Russia

## Overview
> Modern Russia's economic system represents a hybrid of a free market and Soviet-style bureaucratic control, where state machinery and judicial complicity are heavily weaponized to seize and manage private capital.

## Core Concepts
- **The Necessity of State Curators:** Big business cannot exist without state "curators"; enterprises must pay constant bribes to a vast bureaucratic class to survive within the heavily corrupt system.
- **Hostile Takeovers via Telephone Law:** Oligarchs and gangsters execute hostile takeovers of major state assets using armed groups, protected by high-ranking police officials and corrupt judges who issue rapid "rulings-to-order" dictated over the phone (_pozvonochnost_).
- **The Expropriation of Yukos:** When business leaders, such as Mikhail Khodorkovsky, challenge the Kremlin's political monopoly, the state utilizes its punitive apparatus to jail executives, dismantle their corporations, and transfer multi-billion-dollar assets to Kremlin-favored loyalists.
