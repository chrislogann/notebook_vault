# Business Interest In Spain

## Overview
> During the Spanish Civil War, international corporate alliances and domestic financial cartels systematically bypassed democratic neutrality to supply and entrench Francisco Franco's fascist regime.

## Core Concepts
- **Corporate Backing:** Major American and British corporations, including Texaco, Ford, and DuPont, actively boycotted the democratic Spanish Republic while providing the Nationalists with critical resources like oil, military trucks, and bombs on credit.
- **Arms-Trafficking:** Nazi Germany's Hermann Göring exploited the conflict for financial gain, running a secret arms-trafficking racket that sold inferior weapons to the Spanish Republic for hard currency while sending premium weaponry to Franco.
- **Bank Cartelization:** Following his victory, Franco established a legally protected cartel with five major Spanish banks, exchanging political cooperation for a complete ban on domestic competition that lasted until 1962.
- **Architectural Symbol:** This state-backed cartelization made the banking sector, rather than the church, the true architectural symbol of the new Spanish state.
