# 03 Business Interest In Politics

## Overview
> The intersection of business interests and political power represents a structural synthesis where state machinery, financial leverage, and regulatory systems are captured by commercial entities to consolidate wealth and authority.

## Core Concepts
- **Synthesis of Public Office and Corporate Enterprise:** Direct corporate capture occurs when politicians simultaneously serve as directors for the enterprises they regulate, facilitating the manipulation of state assets, treaties, and public lands for private gain.
- **Weaponization of Financial Institutions:** Financial sectors act as battlegrounds for political control, deployed either to entrench authoritarian regimes through cartelization or to actively sabotage revolutionary states through banking blockades.
- **Geopolitical Complicity:** Multinational corporations and foreign empires routinely bypass democratic neutrality laws to supply strategic military and financial aid to favored factions, or to engage in unilateral colonial resource extraction.
- **Revolutionary Backlash:** Oppressive state-enforced exploitation and corporate profiteering inevitably trigger violent, state-sanctioned counter-reactions, leading to the total expropriation of private capital by revolutionary successors or authoritarian regimes.
