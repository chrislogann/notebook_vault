# Franco Rise To Power

## Overview
> General Francisco Franco’s ascent to an absolute dictatorship was achieved through his command of the elite Army of Africa, the fortuitous deaths of his rivals, and the forced amalgamation of disparate nationalist factions.

## Core Concepts
- **Strategic Foundation:** Franco's power was rooted in his leadership of the Army of Africa and the Foreign Legion, leveraging their mercenary professionalism, cult of virility, and brutal colonial tactics to secure military dominance.
- **The Power Vacuum:** The sudden deaths of senior co-conspirators General Sanjurjo and General Mola eliminated Franco's primary rivals, allowing him to position himself as the indispensable "man of destiny" and secure critical Axis logistics.
- **Creation of the Monolith:** To resolve the "cauldron of incompatibilities" within the Nationalist zone, Franco issued a decree forcibly merging the fascist Falange and the ultra-Catholic Carlists into a single state party, the FET.
- **Totalitarian Implementation:** By aligning right-wing ideology, centralism, and authoritarianism with the spiritual justification of the Catholic Church, Franco successfully established a cohesive, absolute dictatorship over "New Spain".
