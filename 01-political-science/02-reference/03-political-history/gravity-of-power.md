# Gravity Of Power

## Overview
> The political lifecycle is governed by the "gravity of power," a predictable trajectory where movements transition from grassroots idealism to systemic corruption, and ultimately, administrative collapse.

## Core Concepts
- **The Dawn (Idealist Catalyst):** Movements rely on grassroots purity and naive optimism to rupture entrenched regimes, creating a "Vacuum of Expectation" characterized by hyper-mobilization and a strict purity mandate.
- **The Realist Inversion:** Upon transitioning to governance, the movement is captured by the "Patronage Trap," displacing original moral visions with tribalism, resource dependency, and the pursuit of political prestige.
- **Weaponization of Ideals:** During consolidation, state actors mask predatory realism behind ideological abstraction, elevating original ideals into unyielding dogmas to justify systemic violence and geopolitical realpolitik.
- **The Void (Administrative Collapse):** Extreme factionalism paralyzes the state's capacity to protect citizens, leading to a culture of impunity where vigilantism and politicized secret police ("Telephone Law") fill the power vacuum.
- **Theoretical Synthesis:** Sustainable statecraft requires a structural balance between an ethical moral anchor and administrative competence to avoid becoming either a heroic failure or a cynical gangster state.
