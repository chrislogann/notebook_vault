# 03 Political History

## Overview
> The historical analysis of political cycles, the consolidation of state authority, and the structural transitions of power from idealistic revolutions to realist administrations.

## Core Concepts
- **The Gravity of Power:** Political movements follow a predictable lifecycle, inevitably pulled from grassroots purity toward the predatory logic and materialist interests of the state.
- **Authoritarian Consolidation:** The study of how military and political leaders exploit crises, power vacuums, and factional alliances to establish absolute dictatorships and monolithic state structures.
- **Institutional Decay:** Historical case studies demonstrate how factionalism and patronage traps hollow out state capacity, leading to administrative collapse and the rise of secret police and vigilantism.
