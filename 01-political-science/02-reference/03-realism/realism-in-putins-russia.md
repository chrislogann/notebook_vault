# Realism In Putins Russia

## Overview
> Realism in modern Russia takes the form of gangster capitalism, where the bureaucratic class leverages state power for systematic extortion, hostile corporate takeovers, and the destruction of political opposition.

## Core Concepts
- **Neo-Soviet Capitalism:** A vast bureaucratic _nomenklatura_ extorts continuous bribes from businesses, forcing owners to realistically accept these payments as a necessary tax for acquiring property and surviving.
- **Hostile Takeovers and Judicial Complicity:** The formal rule of law is bypassed by "telephone law" (_pozvonochnost_), allowing state officials to dictate verdicts to corrupt judges and rubber-stamp armed takeovers of valuable private enterprises.
- **State-Directed Destruction:** The state utilizes ruthless retaliation against business executives who politically challenge the Kremlin (e.g., Mikhail Khodorkovsky), dismantling their companies and transferring multi-billion-dollar assets to loyalists.
