# Realism In Spain

## Overview
> Strategic realism during the Spanish Civil War dictated a ruthless calculus where foreign democracies practiced appeasement, corporations pursued profit, and domestic leaders prioritized absolute authority over revolutionary ideology.

## Core Concepts
- **British Non-Intervention:** The British Foreign Office acted with cold-blooded realism, secretly prepared to sacrifice the Spanish Republic for a stable, neutral Spain under Franco to preserve Mediterranean trade routes.
- **Transnational Corporate Support:** Major American and British corporations bypassed neutrality policies to actively supply Franco's fascist forces with essential credits, fuel, and vehicles, acting on pragmatic materialist interests.
- **Soviet Popular Front Realpolitik:** Stalin ordered the Spanish Communist Party to conceal its ongoing social revolution and defend private property to maintain the illusion of a liberal democracy, seeking rapprochement with Britain and France.
- **Calculated Delays for Personal Power:** General Franco purposefully delayed his military advance on Madrid to relieve the Alcázar of Toledo, calculating that the resulting prestige would make his political leadership unchallengeable.
