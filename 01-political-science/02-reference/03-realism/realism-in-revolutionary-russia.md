# Realism In Revolutionary Russia

## Overview
> Realism during the Russian Revolution manifested as Bolshevik structural ruthlessness, the pragmatic professionalization of the military, and the systemic failure of anti-Bolshevik forces to adapt to peasant demands.

## Core Concepts
- **Bolshevik Structural Ruthlessness:** Vladimir Lenin utilized civil war pragmatically, recognizing that achieving a violent *tabula rasa* (clean slate) was the only realistic way to retain power and completely destroy the old order.
- **Trotsky's Military Realism:** Overcoming ideological opposition, Leon Trotsky recruited Tsarist military specialists and employed "blocking detachments" and executions to enforce iron discipline upon incompetent revolutionaries.
- **The White Army's Realist Failure:** The fragmented anti-Bolshevik White Army lost the civil war largely due to administrative inflexibility, specifically their failure to offer realistic land reforms to the peasantry before it was too late.
- **The Nomenklatura's Material Comforts:** Amidst extreme urban starvation and mass rationing, a privileged class of Bolshevik commissars demonstrated materialist realism by living in abundant comfort using commandeered state resources.
