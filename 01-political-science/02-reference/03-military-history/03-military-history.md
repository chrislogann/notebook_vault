# 03 Military History

## Overview
> The study of armed conflicts in the history of humanity, focusing on the tactical maneuvers, strategic decisions, and operational realities of historical warfare and their socio-political impacts.

## Core Concepts
- **Operational Analysis:** Examining specific military campaigns to understand the evolution of tactics, such as the shift from static positional warfare to mechanized blitzkrieg.
- **Civilian and Societal Impact:** Evaluating how military history intersects with civilian populations, including resistance movements, sieges, starvation, and the collapse of morale.
- **Logistics and Technology:** Understanding the role of technological advancement, air superiority, and logistical supply chains in determining the outcomes of prolonged civil and international conflicts.
