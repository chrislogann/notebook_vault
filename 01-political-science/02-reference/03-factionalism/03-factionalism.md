# 03 Factionalism

## Overview
> The study of internal political tribalism, ideological fragmentation, and the structural consequences of deep-seated divisions within a state or movement.

## Core Concepts
- **Transition to Power:** Movements transitioning from grassroots idealism to governance frequently succumb to internal divisions as they struggle with the realities of administration and power consolidation.
- **Systemic Corruption and Purging:** Factionalism often precipitates political survival superseding the public good, leading to systemic violence and the weaponization of government offices.
- **Administrative Collapse:** Extreme partisan warfare paralyzes the state, rendering it incapable of protecting its citizens and leaving it vulnerable to external exploitation.
