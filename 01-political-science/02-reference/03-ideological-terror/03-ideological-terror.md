# 03 Ideological Terror

## Overview
> The systematic use of state violence, purges, and security organs to enforce ideological conformity, target specific classes or social groups, and consolidate power.

## Core Concepts
- **Systematic Cleansing:** Institutionalized violence is frequently justified through "class enemy" rhetoric and cultivated fears of internal subversion.
- **Spy Mania and Repression:** Paranoia regarding internal subversion is aggressively leveraged by states to build massive security apparatuses and enforce total ideological alignment across the populace.
