# Ideological Terror In Russian Civil War

## Overview
> The systematic use of state violence, purges, and security organs to enforce ideological conformity, target specific classes or social groups, and consolidate power during the Russian Civil War.

## Core Concepts
- **Systematic Cleansing:** Institutionalized violence was repeatedly justified through strict "class enemy" rhetoric and manufactured fears of internal subversion.
- **Spy Mania & Repression:** Paranoia regarding internal subversion was heavily leveraged to build expansive security apparatuses (such as the Cheka) and enforce complete ideological alignment.
