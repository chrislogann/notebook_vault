# Spanish Purge

## Overview
> The systematic political purging during the Spanish Civil War, driven by extreme polarization, the myth of the "fifth column," and institutionalized paranoia that transformed opponents into faceless traitors.

## Core Concepts
- **The "Fifth Column" Myth:** Coined by General Emilio Mola, the assertion of a hidden group of Nationalist sympathizers within Republican Madrid became a primary driver of "spy mania" and internal repression.
- **Axes of Paranoia:** Deep structural frictions over class interests, regional independence, and authoritarianism versus libertarianism rendered the Republic highly susceptible to paranoid decay and factional violence.
- **Institutionalized Repression:** Security forces and political committees utilized widespread arrests, extrajudicial killings (e.g., the Paracuellos massacre), and secret police operations to enforce ideological conformity.
- **Communist Scapegoating:** The Spanish Communist Party (PCE) and Soviet advisers weaponized the "fifth column" narrative to label anti-Stalinist rivals (like the POUM) as fascist provocateurs, justifying severe internal purges.
