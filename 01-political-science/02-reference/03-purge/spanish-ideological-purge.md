# Spanish Ideological Purge

## Overview
> The ideological purging and transformation of the Spanish Falange from a radical, proletarian vanguard into a submissive bureaucratic tool controlled by General Franco's traditionalist military establishment.

## Core Concepts
- **Proletarian Roots and Friction:** The original Falangist "Old Shirts" held a virulent anti-capitalist stance aimed at a "National-Sindicalist" revolution, placing them in direct conflict with the conservative elites and landed aristocracy of the Nationalist coalition.
- **Dilution by Opportunism:** As the sole legal party in the Nationalist zone, the Falange was swamped by an influx of "modern reactionaries" in 1936-1937, diluting its radicalism in favor of Catholic orthodoxy and property preservation.
- **Strategic Neutralization:** General Francisco Franco exploited a leadership vacuum and leveraged the coercive superiority of the Army of Africa to sideline radical leaders like Manuel Hedilla.
- **The Decree of Unification:** In 1937, Franco forcibly amalgamated the radical Falange and the ultra-Catholic Carlists into the FET y de las JONS, effectively decapitating the movement's political autonomy and cementing his absolute control.
