# 03 Purge

## Overview
> The study of political purging, highlighting the subordination of revolutionary idealism to the consolidation of absolute power by centralizing authorities through institutional and extrajudicial means.

## Core Concepts
- **Displacement of Idealism:** Radical and populist movements are systematically dismantled, diluted, or co-opted when leaders view grassroots energy as a threat to their own centralized control.
- **Mechanisms of Neutralization:** Purges are executed through strategic forced institutional mergers, targeted partisan impeachments, violent extrajudicial terror, and bitter patronage feuds.
- **Administrative Consequences:** The intense pursuit of political survival and factional supremacy often paralyzes state machinery, leaving governments powerless to protect citizens from systemic violence or monolithic authoritarianism.
