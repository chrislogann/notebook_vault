# Purge In History

## Overview
> A comparative analysis of political purging in Spain and Kansas, demonstrating how revolutionary grassroots idealism is systematically subordinated to consolidate absolute power by a centralizing authority.

## Core Concepts
- **Displacement of Revolutionary Idealism:** Radical and populist movements, such as the Falangist "Old Shirts" and the Wyandotte Convention farmers, are dismantled when leaders view their original, independent energy as a threat to systemic control.
- **Mechanisms of Neutralization:** Leaders execute purges through forced institutional mergers (e.g., Franco's Decree of Unification) or through bitter patronage feuds and targeted partisan impeachments (e.g., General Lane's tactics in Kansas).
- **Administrative Paralysis:** The obsession with factional warfare and political survival supersedes the public good, resulting in powerless states that leave citizens defenseless against marauding border violence or monolithic authoritarianism.
