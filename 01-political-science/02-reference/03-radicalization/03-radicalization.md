# 03 Radicalization

## Overview
> The study of how structural political instability, military intervention, and the hollowing of the political center lead to the empowerment and arming of extreme ideological factions.

## Core Concepts
- **The Hollowed Centre:** The collapse of moderate, centrist governance creates a "Manichaean environment" where ideological dogma and class hatred completely replace nuanced political compromise.
- **The Faustian Pact:** Desperate centrist governments often arm radical militias to suppress military coups, committing institutional suicide by permanently destroying the state's monopoly on force.
- **The Victorious Sword:** The ultimate consequence of this structural instability is the permanent transfer of power from formal state structures to radicalized, militant extremes.
