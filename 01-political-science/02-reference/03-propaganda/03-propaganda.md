# 03 Propaganda

## Overview
> The study of how manichaean propaganda, paranoid fabrications, and the cynical manipulation of public opinion are actively deployed as primary instruments of state survival, military mobilization, and strategic deception.

## Core Concepts
- **Atrocity Fabrications:** Regimes manipulate global opinion and polarize society by manufacturing atrocity narratives and spreading highly sensationalized, unsubstantiated stories to justify conflict.
- **Driver of Military Strategy:** Exaggerated, triumphalist propaganda often traps leadership in their own lies, leading to disastrous tactical decisions and useless "prestige operations" strictly to sustain public illusions of victory.
- **Institutionalized Paranoia:** States utilize fabricated conspiracy theories and highly theatrical show trials as a blunt instrument of social terror and ideological control.
- **Cold War Rhetoric:** Bellicose propaganda and active deception can incite catastrophic disconnects—such as broadcasting the illusion of Western "liberation" to incite resistance, while private policy remains focused on containment and deniability.
