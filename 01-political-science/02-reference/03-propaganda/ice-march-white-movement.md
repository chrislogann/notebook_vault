# Ice March

## Overview
> The 1918 military retreat of the Volunteer Army in South Russia, which was retroactively converted into a foundational political myth and epic of defeat for the White movement.

## Core Concepts
- **Myth-Making in Civil War:** The event demonstrates how military failures or heroic retreats are deliberately reframed into nationalistic or ideological myths to build movement identity and sanitize struggle.
- **Narrative Framing and Survival:** It serves as a primary case study in how civil wars are narrated and memorialized to mobilize public support, functioning as the primary ideological glue for a movement lacking a cohesive political program.
