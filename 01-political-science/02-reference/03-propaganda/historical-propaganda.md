# Historical Propaganda

## Overview
> An analysis of how propaganda and information control have been historically weaponized to manipulate domestic populations, deceive foreign observers, and drive military strategies across twentieth-century conflicts.

## Core Concepts
- **The Manipulation of Global Opinion:** During the Spanish Civil War, extremes polarized society by manufacturing atrocity narratives—such as the Nationalist crusade myth and the disinformation surrounding Guernica—to strip opponents of their humanity.
- **Triumphalism and Military Strategy:** Exaggerated, triumphalist propaganda can trap military leadership, forcing them to repeatedly sacrifice troops in slow-moving "prestige operations" purely to sustain the public illusion of victory.
- **Stalinist Paranoia and the Show Trial:** Postwar states utilized fabricated conspiracies (e.g., the POUM-Gestapo narrative) and theatrical show trials as blunt instruments of social terror, using blatant lies to achieve a disorienting effect on the populace.
- **Cold War Rhetoric and Tactical Incitement:** Western psychological operations, such as Radio Free Europe, broadcasted the illusion of "rollback" and "liberation," actively inciting desperate nations (like Hungary in 1956) to commit suicide in hopeless struggles while actual policy prioritized plausible deniability.
