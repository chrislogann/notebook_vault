# Politicized Intelligence

## Overview
> Politicized intelligence communities operate as the ultimate praetorian guards of ruling regimes, weaponized to enforce ideological conformity, execute political purges, orchestrate resource extraction, and systematically terrorize domestic populations.

## Core Concepts
- **Industrialized Torture and Non-Persons:** Agencies such as the AVO, SIM, Cheka, and FSB establish off-the-grid confinement spaces to deploy systemic torture, discarding legal evidence in favor of class-based or ideological judgements and "disappearing" individuals.
- **Infiltration and "Telephone Law":** Politicized intelligence networks systematically capture the judiciary and state machinery, utilizing direct executive dictation (e.g., _pozvonochnost_ in Russia) to rubber-stamp hostile takeovers and eliminate independent oversight.
- **Fabricating Conspiracies:** To maintain unchecked authority, these networks invent highly theatrical conspiracy theories—from Stalinist show trials in Hungary to the POUM-Gestapo fabrication in Spain—disorienting populations and justifying permanent emergencies.
- **Recruitment of Societal "Dregs":** Agencies consistently bypass professional codes by systematically recruiting criminals and sociopaths (e.g., former Arrow Cross enforcers in the AVO) who are highly efficient at intimidation and easily controlled via blackmail.
- **Active Measures and Deception:** Intelligence services execute complex information-control campaigns and active deceptions, calmly lying to foreign observers and domestic citizens alike to cover up state-sanctioned atrocities or prevent effective resistance.
