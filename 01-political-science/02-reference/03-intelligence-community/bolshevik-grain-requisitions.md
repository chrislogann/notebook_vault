# Bolshevik Grain Requisitions

## Overview
> The enforcement of state grain requisitions by security apparatuses (such as the Cheka) linked institutionalized terror directly to economic control and urban survival.

## Core Concepts
- **Economic Dimension of Terror:** The state utilized forced food extraction and orchestrated starvation as primary tools for both political and social control over the populace.
- **Institutionalized Security Organs:** Security agencies transitioned rapidly from temporary emergency measures to permanent organs dedicated to enforcing total economic and political obedience.
