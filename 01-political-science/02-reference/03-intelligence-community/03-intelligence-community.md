# 03 Intelligence Community

## Overview
> The study of politicized intelligence communities that, rather than functioning as neutral arbiters of national security, operate as the ultimate praetorian guards of ruling regimes to enforce ideological conformity, execute political purges, and terrorize domestic populations.

## Core Concepts
- **Institutionalized Repression:** Intelligence apparatuses establish specialized, off-the-grid spaces to deploy physical and psychological torture, extracting fabricated confessions to serve state narratives.
- **State Infiltration:** Agencies systematically capture and subordinate the judiciary, police force, and military command, operating above the law to dictate verdicts and consolidate authoritarian power.
- **Information Control and Active Measures:** Regimes utilize these communities to execute active-deception measures, fabricate elaborate conspiracy theories, and maintain a permanent state of emergency.
