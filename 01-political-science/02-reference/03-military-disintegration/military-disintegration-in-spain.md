# Military Disintegration In Spain

## Overview
> During the Spanish Civil War, the Republican military disintegrated due to a "cauldron of incompatibilities," contrasting sharply with the Nationalist forces that maintained cohesion through forced political unification and rigid authoritarian structures.

## Core Concepts
- **Axes of Conflict:** The Spanish military collapse was driven by three fundamental antagonisms: class interests, authoritarianism versus libertarianism, and centralism versus regionalism.
- **The Republic's Cauldron of Incompatibilities:** Republican military efficiency was paralyzed by competing organizational philosophies, pitting the centralist "Prussian" bureaucracy of the Communists against the decentralized "gimnasia revolucionaria" of the Anarchists.
- **Civil War within the Civil War:** Republican cohesion was further undermined by the Comintern's strategy of infiltration and the violent elimination of anti-Stalinist political rivals, destroying republican idealism in favor of political control.
- **Nationalist Cohesion:** Conversely, the Nationalist block achieved unity by forcibly amalgamating disparate factions (Falange and Carlists) into a singular entity and utilizing the Army as the unquestioned "spinal column" of the nation.
