# 03 Military Disintegration

## Overview
> The study of how professional military structures, command hierarchies, and operational discipline collapse under the weight of political polarization, ideological friction, and revolutionary pressures.

## Core Concepts
- **Command Breakdown:** The systematic destruction of traditional military command hierarchies through the empowerment of political committees and the subversion of officer authority.
- **Ideological Friction:** The erosion of military effectiveness when internal "civil wars within civil wars" and structural incompatibilities prioritize political purity over tactical cohesion.
- **Forced Amalgamation vs. Fragmentation:** The contrast between successful forced unification of disparate military factions by authoritarian structures and the catastrophic fragmentation caused by competing regional and libertarian interests.
