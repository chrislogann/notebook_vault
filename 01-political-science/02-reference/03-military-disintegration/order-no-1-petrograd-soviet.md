# Order No 1 Petrograd Soviet

## Overview
> Issued in March 1917, Petrograd Soviet Order No. 1 served as the specific legal mechanism for Russian military collapse by empowering soldiers' committees and mandating that military units obey political commands over officers.

## Core Concepts
- **Destruction of Command Hierarchy:** The order systematically dismantled traditional military command hierarchies, severely eroding officer authority and operational discipline on the front lines.
- **Politicized Vanguard:** It facilitated the rapid radicalization of military units and naval personnel (e.g., the Baltic Fleet), transforming them from professional soldiers into revolutionary enforcers.
- **Catalyst for Breakdown:** By prioritizing political commands over military directives, the order acted as a foundational catalyst for the broader disintegration of the Russian imperial military apparatus.
