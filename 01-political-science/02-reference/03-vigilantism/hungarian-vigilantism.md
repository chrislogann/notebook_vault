# Hungarian Vigilantism

## Overview
> During the 1956 Hungarian Revolution, street-level vigilantism erupted as spontaneous lynch mobs delivered brutal instant justice against the Soviet-backed secret police (AVO) in response to civilian massacres.

## Core Concepts
- **Atrocities as Catalysts:** Mob violence was triggered by specific, horrific massacres where AVO officers opened fire on peaceful protesters, provoking armed miners and citizens to lay siege to security headquarters.
- **Gruesome Retribution:** Insurgents exacted extreme vengeance at sites like Republic Square and Mosonmagyaróvár, capturing, beating, burning, or hanging secret police officers.
- **The Code of Blood Money:** Despite chaotic frenzy, crowds adhered to a symbolic code, leaving victims' cash untouched on the ground to signal that money was tainted "blood money" and refuse simple thievery.
- **Revolutionary Condemnation:** Leaders of the revolution, including associates of Imre Nagy, strongly condemned the lynchings, urging formal court trials to prevent the movement's purity from being defiled.
