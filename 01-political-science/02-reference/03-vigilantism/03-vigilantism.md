# 03 Vigilantism

## Overview
> The study of extralegal justice, examining how societies witness the rise of mob justice, paramilitary raids, and state-sanctioned retribution when the formal rule of law collapses, becomes corrupted, or is perceived as unjust.

## Core Concepts
- **Spontaneous Mob Justice:** When formal oppression becomes unbearable, citizens frequently form spontaneous lynch mobs to deliver instant street-level justice against instruments of state terror, balancing brutality with strict symbolic codes.
- **Extralegal Courts and Border Wars:** Biased judicial systems often force marginalized populations to bypass official courts and establish extralegal jurisdictions, which frequently degenerate into full-scale border warfare and pure criminality.
- **Sanctified Gangsterism:** During civil conflict, political purges and extrajudicial execution squads are leveraged by political extremes to conduct systematic ideological cleansing.
- **State-Sanctioned Lynch Law:** The most insidious evolution of vigilantism is top-down state sponsorship, where arbitrary retaliation, impunity, and custom law effectively replace the formal rule of law.
