# Vigilantism In Spain

## Overview
> During the Spanish Civil War, extralegal violence and "sanctified gangsterism" became primary instruments for systematic ideological cleansing and terror across both Nationalist and Republican zones.

## Core Concepts
- **Catalyst of Retaliation:** The extrajudicial assassination of right-wing leader José Calvo Sotelo following left-wing provocations provided Nationalists with the final justification for their pre-planned military rebellion.
- **Nationalist Sanctified Gangsterism:** Upper-class _señoritos_ formed mobile execution squads that abducted and murdered liberals, leftists, and cultural figures like Federico García Lorca with absolute impunity.
- **Republican Red Terror:** The collapse of state authority in Republican territory triggered revolutionary _checas_ and tribunals responsible for tens of thousands of extrajudicial executions and mass prison slaughters.
- **Moral Resistance:** The horrific scale of extrajudicial slaughter prompted figures like Basque philosopher Miguel de Unamuno to publicly rebuke the senseless glorification of death at the University of Salamanca.
