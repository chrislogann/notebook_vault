# State Sanctioned Vigilantism

## Overview
> The institutionalization of extrajudicial retaliation and state-sponsored "lynch law" as a structural mechanism for maintaining control, eliminating political opposition, and replacing the formal rule of law.

## Core Concepts
- **Top-Down Modeling:** State-sponsored vigilantism is modeled from the highest levels of government, with leadership setting precedents of arbitrary retaliation (e.g., the destruction of Yukos) to bypass formal legal constraints.
- **Military Impunity and Customary Law:** In conflicts like the second Chechen war, military atrocities and murders (such as the Budanov case and Dai Massacre) were justified by courts under personal retribution, establishing a culture of absolute impunity.
- **Infection of Law Enforcement and Commerce:** The normalization of lynch law infected civilian policing—manifested in fabricated drug charges against minorities—and allowed oligarchs to deploy private armies for violent corporate takeovers.
