# Kansas Vigilantism

## Overview
> Territorial and early statehood Kansas witnessed extensive extralegal justice, as Free-State settlers bypassed pro-slavery courts to establish vigilante tribunals, escalating into border warfare and opportunistic criminality.

## Core Concepts
- **Roots of Extralegal Courts:** Facing pro-slavery territorial courts that systematically stripped them of land claims, Free-State settlers and leaders bypassed official institutions entirely to establish independent extralegal courts.
- **Jayhawking and Border Warfare:** Militant abolitionists like James Montgomery and John Brown led cross-border raids and enforced violations of the Fugitive Slave Law, plunging the region into continuous turmoil.
- **Descent into Criminality:** During the Civil War, political vigilance masked pure criminality, with irregular units degenerating into horse thieves and robber bands that preyed upon even fellow Union sympathizers.
- **Political Fracture:** Unchecked vigilantism severely divided the Free-State Party into a radical wing that defended the Jayhawkers and a conservative faction led by Governor Charles Robinson that condemned them as revolutionaries.
