# Foreign Intervention In Civil War

## Overview
> The involvement of external powers in a domestic conflict, turning local political or civil strife into a broader geopolitical proxy war.

## Core Concepts
- **Proxy Conflict:** External powers supply aid, troops, or logistics to advance their own strategic interests rather than the interests of the local populace.
- **Fractured Movements:** Foreign aid attached to specific political conditions often splits the internal unity of revolutionary or defensive forces, forcing them to prioritize external demands over internal cohesion.
