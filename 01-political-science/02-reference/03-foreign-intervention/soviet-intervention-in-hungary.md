# Soviet Intervention In Hungary

## Overview
> In post-WWII Hungary, the intersection of business and politics took the form of rapid state-sanctioned nationalization and direct colonial exploitation orchestrated by the intervening Soviet Union.

## Core Concepts
- **Salami Tactics and Nationalization:** Communist leader Mátyás Rákosi utilized "salami tactics" to gradually slice away democratic opposition, escalating from demands for government control to the outright nationalization of the country's largest financial institutions.
- **Colonial Resource Extraction:** The Soviet Union treated Hungary as a classic colonial possession, pillaging its vast uranium reserves under the guise of a secret joint company, keeping extraction terms so lopsided and secretive that Hungary’s own Atomic Energy Commission was left completely in the dark.
