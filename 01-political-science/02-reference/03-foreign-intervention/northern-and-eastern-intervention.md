# Northern And Eastern Intervention

## Overview
> Allied military interventions in Murmansk, Arkhangel, and Vladivostok during the Russian Civil War were heavily driven by World War I supply security and front preservation.

## Core Concepts
- **Great War Complexity:** This dynamic differs from standard proxy conflicts by combining world-war strategic logistics directly with domestic civil war intervention.
- **Multi-Front External Strain:** Geographically distinct interventions by external forces drastically impacted regional political unity and shaped long-term foreign relations.
