# Foreign Intervention History

## Overview
> A comparative analysis demonstrating how internal struggles in Spain and Kansas were overtaken by the agendas of out-of-state actors, transforming vulnerable regions into proxy battlegrounds for ideological dominance, political control, and economic gain.

## Core Concepts
- **Proxy Warfare and Military Interference:** External actors hijacked local conflicts to wage broader wars; Spain became a testing ground for Axis modern weaponry and Soviet intervention, while Kansas suffered guerrilla warfare driven by out-of-state "Border Ruffians" and federal politicians.
- **Political Subversion and Ideological Control:** Foreign forces heavily manipulated internal politics; the Comintern dismantled independent leftist factions in Spain with Stalinist terror, while Eastern politicians and financiers dictated political platforms and Senate elections in Kansas.
- **Severe Economic Exploitation:** Both vulnerable local governments were mercilessly exploited for profit; the Spanish Republic surrendered 510 tons of gold to Moscow and saw its minerals extracted by Nazi monopolies, while Kansas was forced to sell its state bonds at a massive loss in a corrupt federal scheme.
