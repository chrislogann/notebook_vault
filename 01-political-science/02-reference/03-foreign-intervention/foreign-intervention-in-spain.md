# Foreign Intervention In Spain

## Overview
> Out-of-state actors fundamentally transformed the Spanish Civil War from an internal military coup into a "world war by proxy," utilizing the conflict as a testing ground for modern weaponry, a political chessboard, and a distraction for broader geopolitical ambitions.

## Core Concepts
- **Axis Military Intervention and the "Testing Ground":** Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy provided the critical military lifeline for General Franco's Nationalist forces, using the war to test rapid _blitzkrieg_ tactics and the psychological effects of aerial terrorism on civilians.
- **Soviet Military Aid and Communist Subversion:** The Soviet Union supplied the Republic with weapons tied to severe political strings, utilizing the Comintern to organize International Brigades while NKVD operatives infiltrated the Republican army to dismantle independent leftist factions.
- **The Farce of Western "Non-Intervention":** The arms embargo enforced by Western democracies fatally crippled the Republic, yet entirely failed to stop massive Axis shipments to the Nationalists or prevent American and British corporations from financing Franco on credit.
- **Economic Exploitation:** Both factions mortgaged Spain's future to out-of-state actors; the Republic transferred 510 tons of its gold reserves to Moscow, while Germany established monopolies to extract Spanish mineral wealth to feed its rearmament program.
