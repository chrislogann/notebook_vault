# 03 Foreign Intervention

## Overview
> The phenomenon where domestic conflicts or internal struggles are completely overtaken by the agendas of out-of-state actors, transforming vulnerable regions into proxy battlegrounds for ideological dominance, political control, and economic gain.

## Core Concepts
- **Proxy Warfare and Military Interference:** External actors hijack local conflicts to wage broader wars, using proxy regions as testing grounds for modern weaponry, tactics, or to fulfill broader geopolitical ambitions.
- **Political Subversion and Ideological Control:** Foreign forces heavily manipulate internal politics to align with their own ideologies, actively shaping local political platforms, supplying conditional aid, or infiltrating local secret police apparatuses.
- **Severe Economic Exploitation:** Vulnerable local governments are mercilessly exploited for profit by the external powers claiming to assist them, frequently resulting in mortgaged national futures, resource monopolies, or corrupt financial schemes.
