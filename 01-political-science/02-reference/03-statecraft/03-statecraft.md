# 03 Statecraft

## Overview
> The art and practice of government, encompassing the strategic integration of public office with commercial interests, geopolitical realpolitik, ideological abstraction, and the exercise of force to control social orders.

## Core Concepts
- **Integration of Capital and Public Office:** Statecraft frequently dissolves the boundary between political actors and commercial promoters, enabling widespread corporate capture and speculative land acquisitions.
- **Geopolitical Realpolitik:** At the international level, statecraft operates as a cold-blooded calculus where materialist self-interest, spheres of influence, and survival take absolute precedence over democratic ideals.
- **Weaponization of Ideology:** Regimes utilize idealism as a primary political fuel, but consolidate power by transforming those ideals into rigid, unyielding dogmas that justify systemic violence and totalitarian control.
- **Force as an Extension of Governance:** Statecraft relies on violence and coercive apparatuses—such as the Cheka or military rising—to achieve complete command over the social order and establish an absolute clean slate (_tabula rasa_).
