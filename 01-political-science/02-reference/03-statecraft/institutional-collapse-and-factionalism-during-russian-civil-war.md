# Institutional Collapse And Factionalism During Russian Civil War

## Overview
> The rapid disintegration of state authority creates a severe power vacuum where internal tribalism and factionalism prevent new governance structures from stabilizing.

## Core Concepts
- **Predictable Trajectory:** Political movements demonstrate a progressive degradation, moving predictably from grassroots idealism to institutional corruption and administrative paralysis.
- **Power Vacuums and Dual Power:** Internal friction between competing administrative authorities leaves states highly vulnerable to external exploitation and totalizing violence.
