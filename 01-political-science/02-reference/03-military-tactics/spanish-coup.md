# Spanish Coup

## Overview
> The July 1936 Spanish Nationalist Coup was a highly coordinated military conspiracy driven by a traditionalist coalition seeking to resolve a structural crisis of identity and state authority.

## Core Concepts
- **Axes of Conflict:** The coup was the violent culmination of historic struggles between centralism and regionalism, authoritarianism and libertarianism, and deeply entrenched class warfare.
- **Colonial Vanguard:** The Army of Africa served as the indispensable base for the rebellion, utilizing the Foreign Legion's mercenary professionalism and psychological stealth tactics to overwhelm disorganized republican militias.
- **Naval Mutiny & The Axis Bridge:** The rebels' failure to secure the navy due to a mutiny of lower-deck ratings was bypassed by the first major military airlift in history, heavily supported by Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy.
- **Territorial Partition:** The 48-hour struggle left the country physically divided, transforming what was intended to be a rapid coup into a prolonged war defined by systemic "cleansing" and total elimination.
