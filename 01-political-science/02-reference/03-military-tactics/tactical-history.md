# Tactical History

## Overview
> The "Doctrine of the Duel" establishes a strategic architecture for joint military tactics, differentiating the political essence of war from the mechanical execution of warfare to guide Joint Force operations.

## Core Concepts
- **The Social Trinity and The Duel:** War operates as a violent clash of wills where commanders must manage the shifting interplay of emotion (the people), chance and uncertainty (the military), and reason (the government).
- **Strategies of Force:** Military tactics fall on an operational spectrum between Annihilation (the physical destruction of enemy capacity) and Erosion (exhausting the enemy's will to resist and raising the costs of conflict).
- **Hierarchy of Execution:** Combat execution is divided into Strategic (national policy), Operational (campaign sequencing), and Tactical (small-unit maneuvers) levels, though modern information environments increasingly cause single tactical actions to have immediate strategic implications.
- **Engine of Combat Power:** Synergistic combat power is generated by integrating seven joint functions: Command and Control, Intelligence, Fires, Movement and Maneuver, Protection, Sustainment, and Information.
- **Instruments of National Power (DIME):** Tactical success is void unless it directly aligns with Diplomatic, Informational, Military, and Economic national strategies to achieve a broader political end state.
