# Kornilov Coup In Revolutionary Russia

## Overview
> The August 1917 Kornilov Affair demonstrated the structural instability of Dual Power in Russia, catalyzing the hollowing of the political centre and the rise of military and proletarian radicalization.

## Core Concepts
- **Structural Instability:** The Provisional Government relied on a "Faustian pact" with the radical left, creating a "cauldron of incompatibilities" that inadvertently facilitated the bolshevization of the state.
- **The Africanista Doctrine:** The Russian officer corps operated under a traditionalist military creed, viewing civilian politicians with contempt and positioning themselves as a warrior elite necessary to restore the "spine of the state".
- **Logistical Sabotage:** The military's centralized brute-force advance was defeated not by a rival army, but by the decentralized logistical paralysis and sabotage executed by railway workers and proletarian resistance.
- **The Victorious Sword:** The failure of the coup and the subsequent arrest of the High Command completely extinguished military-civilian trust, ensuring the permanent transfer of state power to the Bolshevik militias.
