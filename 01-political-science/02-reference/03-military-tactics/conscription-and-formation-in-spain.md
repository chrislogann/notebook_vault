# Conscription And Formation In Spain

## Overview
> The Spanish Second Republic's war effort evolved from disorganized, decentralized revolutionary militias into a formal, centralized "People's Army" out of strict military necessity.

## Core Concepts
- **Collapse of the Regular Army:** The republican government dissolved the regular army by decree, destroying its administrative framework for centralized defense and shifting sovereignty to trade union militias.
- **Revolutionary Gymnastics:** Initial defense relied on spontaneous militias (like the CNT-FAI) that completely lacked technical integration and professional military education, prioritizing ideological fervor over tactical cohesion.
- **Transition to the People's Army:** Driven by the Communist Party and Soviet influence, the Republic forcibly re-established traditional hierarchy, professional discipline, and centralized strategic command to face the Nationalist regulars.
- **Structural Incompatibility:** The Republic remained a "cauldron of incompatibilities," constantly struggling to harmonize state centralism with regionalist and libertarian autonomy, which fatally undermined its military efficiency.
