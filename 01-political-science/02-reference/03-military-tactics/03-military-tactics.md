# 03 Military Tactics

## Overview
> The strategic architecture of joint military tactics, distinguishing the political essence of war from the mechanical execution of warfare to generate combat power and unified action.

## Core Concepts
- **Theoretical Nexus:** War is fundamentally socially sanctioned violence to achieve a political purpose, demanding that commanders navigate the non-rational variables of emotion and chance alongside rational governance.
- **Operational Spectrum:** Tactics span both traditional warfare (aiming for domination and annihilation) and irregular warfare (aiming for legitimacy, influence, and the erosion of enemy will).
- **Execution Hierarchy:** A cognitive framework links tactical actions (small-unit engagements) to operational campaigns and ultimately to strategic national policy objectives.
- **Unified Action:** Combat power is maximized through the integration of seven joint functions (e.g., C2, Intelligence, Fires, Sustainment) and the synchronization of military operations with interorganizational partners.
