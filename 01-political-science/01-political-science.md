# 01 Political Science

## Overview
> Political science, in its operational application, is the clinical study of the "gravity of power"—the unyielding and predictable forces that govern the survival of states, the decay of institutions, and the movement of political actors.

## Core Concepts
- **Strategic Realism:** A cold-blooded calculus where geopolitical survival and materialist self-interest systematically dismantle or exploit democratic and moral ideals.
- **Political Idealism:** The psychological fuel for statecraft, which ranges from grassroots naive sentimentalism to totalizing doctrinal ruthlessness.
- **The Political Lifecycle:** The predictable evolution of movements as they succumb to the gravity of power, transitioning from grassroots purity to institutional displacement, realist inversion, and eventual administrative collapse.
- **The Architecture of Power:** Regimes systematically weaponize mythology to sanitize state violence, synthesize capital with public office to capture economic resources, and utilize politicized intelligence as a praetorian guard to enforce ideological conformity.
- **Survival Equilibrium:** To avoid devolving into a cynical gangster state or a heroic failure, a state must maintain administrative competence, a moral anchor, structural vigilance against ambition, and institutional trust.
