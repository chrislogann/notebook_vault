# International Business In Spain

## Overview
> During the Spanish Civil War, domestic and international business networks actively bypassed neutrality laws to finance and supply the Nationalist victory.

## Core Concepts
- **Funding the Right:** The Nationalist conspiracy was heavily bankrolled by domestic millionaires, landowners, and corporations.
- **Foreign Corporate Backing:** Despite non-intervention policies, major US and UK corporations (like Texaco, Ford, and DuPont) actively boycotted the Republic while extending credit, oil, and weapons to Franco's forces.
- **Profiteering on Both Sides:** Elements like Nazi Germany's Hermann Göring ran secret arms-trafficking rackets to profit off both the Republic and the Nationalists.
- **The Post-War Bank-State Alliance:** The victorious regime established an iron-clad, legally protected monopoly with five major Spanish banks, making the bank the architectural symbol of the new Spain.
