# Out Of State Intervention In Kansas

## Overview
> Out-of-state actors and Eastern capital served as proxy forces shaping the political and military birth of Kansas.

## Core Concepts
- **Border Warfare and Southern Secessionists:** Missouri border ruffians and Confederate forces attacked Kansas settlers to influence its stance on slavery and exploit resources.
- **Eastern Political and Financial Influence:** Prominent national leaders and Eastern financiers controlled Kansas elections and funnelled funds through famine relief organizations for political gain.
- **Federal Military Patronage and Interference:** Federal authorities disrupted internal governance by authorizing independent military recruitment, undermining the state's governor.
- **Economic Exploitation by Out-of-State Speculators:** Out-of-state speculators orchestrated corrupt bond arbitrage schemes to profit immensely off the financially desperate new state.
