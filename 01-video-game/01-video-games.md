# 01 Video Games

## Overview
> A broad domain encompassing the study, strategic analysis, and application of methodologies within interactive digital entertainment and game systems.

## Core Concepts
- **Strategic Frameworks:** Applying external cognitive, political, and military models to master the constraints and variables of game engines.
- **Game Mechanics Analysis:** Deconstructing rulesets, AI behavior, and resource pipelines to optimize gameplay and achieve definitive victory conditions.
