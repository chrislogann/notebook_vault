# Civ 1 Computational Thinking

## Overview
> A strategic framework for Civilization 1 that applies computational abstraction and Chinese military doctrines to treat the game as a state-machine logic puzzle optimized for systemic dominance.

## Core Concepts
- **Active Defense and Abstraction:** The grand strategist maintains a defensive political profile while using preemptive tactical strikes to ensure "buffer-zone maintenance," treating game elements like shields and science as raw variables in a state-level power equation.
- **System Warfare Decomposition:** The civilization is managed as a "System of Systems" comprising Command (Capital), Strike (Military), Information (Diplomacy), Intelligence, and Support (Logistics/Irrigation) networks.
- **Rural Encirclement and Expansion:** Prioritizing a decentralized network of small cities exploits the "Infinite City Sprawl" (ICS) mechanic, maximizing science throughput and managing corruption efficiently.
- **The Three Warfares:** Diplomatic hegemony is achieved through Legal Warfare (treaties to freeze AI), Psychological Warfare (high power graph ranking for deterrence), and Public Opinion Warfare (building Wonders).
