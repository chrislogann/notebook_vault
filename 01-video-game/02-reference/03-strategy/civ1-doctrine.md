# Civ 1 Doctrine

## Overview
> An advanced operational doctrine for Civilization 1 that utilizes pattern recognition, civil-military fusion, and asymmetric targeting to systematically dismantle AI opponents.

## Core Concepts
- **Comprehensive National Power (CNP):** Players use abstraction to evaluate their overall strength, combining Hard Power (military units, gold) and Soft Power (science, population, Wonders) to execute a Minimax evaluation that minimizes the opponent's potential.
- **Threat Classification & Environment:** Threats are classified from "Military Risk" to "Large-Scale War" using Russian Military Doctrine, while High-Value Operational Environments like coastal chokepoints and mountain buffers are identified to deny enemy access.
- **Asymmetric System Degradation:** The AI is decomposed into functional subsystems so players can execute targeted strikes, such as using Horsemen to pillage irrigation (Support Degradation) or eliminating scouts to reset the fog-of-war (Information Blinding).
- **Tactical Execution & Mobilization:** Executing "People's War Principles for Sieges" involves algorithmic steps like starving defenders via pillaging, alongside performing heuristic audits every five turns to verify military readiness and system health.
