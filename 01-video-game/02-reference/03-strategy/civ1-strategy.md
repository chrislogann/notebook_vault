# Civ 1 Strategy

## Overview
> A foundational checklist and tactical guide for expanding, building, and developing a competitive civilization while assessing strategic, economic, and environmental realities.

## Core Concepts
- **Strategic Evaluation:** Players must continuously assess if their industrial output meets strategic needs, define the specific role of each city (military, industrial, economic, or political), and ensure the chosen government meets output expectations.
- **Military and Operational Focus:** General strategy requires defending cities with garrisons and air support, patrolling borders with highly mobile units like cavalry or armor, and defining a clear path to either a space race or military victory.
- **Hybrid Warfare Tactics:** Advanced endgame tactics include weaponizing the pollution mechanic to trigger global warming or utilizing scorched earth strategies against adversaries.
