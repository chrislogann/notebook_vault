# 03 Strategy

## Overview
> The study and application of strategic planning, resource management, and tactical execution within complex video game environments, focusing heavily on systems thinking and military doctrines.

## Core Concepts
- **Active Defense and Abstraction:** Utilizing proactive tactical offense to secure borders while analyzing the game state through abstracted variables like Comprehensive National Power (CNP).
- **Systemic Decomposition:** Breaking down an opponent into critical subsystems (Command, Strike, Information, Intelligence, and Support) to identify vulnerabilities and direct kinetic operations.
- **Strategic Deterrence and Warfare:** Applying multi-domain tactics such as the "Three Warfares" (Legal, Psychological, Public Opinion) and nuclear deterrence to achieve hegemony and victory without unnecessary battle.
