# 01 Computer Science

## Overview
> A foundational academic discipline dealing with computation, including the theoretical foundations, practical implementation, and behavioral models of information processing systems.

## Core Concepts
- **Computational Theory:** Defines the absolute limits of what computers can calculate, governed by concepts like Turing Machines and the Halting Problem.
- **Algorithms and Data Structures:** The optimal procedures and structural implementations (like Hash Maps and Trees) used to manipulate data efficiently.
- **Interdisciplinary Subfields:** Expands into specialized domains including Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, and Quantum Computing.
