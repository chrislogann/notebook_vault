# Psu

## Overview
> The Power Supply Unit (PSU) is a critical component that converts alternating current (AC) from the power grid into stable, regulated direct current (DC) for computer components.

## Core Concepts
- PSUs step down high AC voltage into crucial lower DC voltages, prioritizing the 12V bus which powers high-draw components like the CPU and GPU.
- Efficiency ratings (e.g., 80 PLUS Gold) measure the effectiveness of the AC-to-DC conversion, directly impacting heat generation and system longevity.
- Proper wattage selection must account for peak load draws, allowing 150% to 200% overhead capacity to maintain system stability during transient spikes.
