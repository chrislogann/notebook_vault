# Ssd

## Overview
> Solid State Drives (SSDs) are non-volatile storage devices utilizing NAND flash memory cells and sophisticated controller algorithms to deliver extremely low-latency data retention.

## Core Concepts
- SSDs completely eliminate mechanical moving parts, replacing them with electronic cells grouped into blocks and managed by a dedicated controller processor.
- The drive controller executes vital maintenance algorithms—like wear leveling and garbage collection—to distribute write operations and maximize the drive's finite lifespan.
- Modern form factors, such as M.2 NVMe, utilize the high-speed PCIe bus to bypass older SATA bottlenecks, delivering multi-gigabyte per second sequential throughput.
