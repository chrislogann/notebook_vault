# Decomposition

## Overview
> Decomposition is the first step of computational thinking, involving the breakdown of a complex problem or system into smaller, manageable, and understandable parts.

## Core Concepts
- **Simplifying Complexity:** Dealing with multiple stages of a large problem at once is overwhelming; decomposition translates massive challenges into a series of highly specific, easily solvable tasks.
- **Granular Focus:** Breaking a problem down allows each individual component to be examined, designed, and solved in much greater detail.
- **Systemic Understanding:** Comprehending a massive system is significantly more straightforward when analyzed through its constituent, separated parts.
