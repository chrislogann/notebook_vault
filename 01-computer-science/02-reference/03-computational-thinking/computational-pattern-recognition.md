# Computational Pattern Recognition

## Overview
> Pattern recognition involves identifying similarities or shared characteristics among decomposed problems to reuse successful strategies and solve complex issues more efficiently.

## Core Concepts
- **The Sequence:** Pattern recognition depends on prior decomposition; a complex problem must first be deconstructed before shared attributes can be identified among its smaller parts.
- **Universal vs. Specifics:** Recognizing patterns requires identifying the fundamental, universal characteristics shared by all instances, separate from their unique specific details.
- **Efficiency Multiplier:** Identifying patterns prevents treating every small problem as entirely unique, allowing developers to apply established solutions to a wide range of similar problems rather than building from scratch.
