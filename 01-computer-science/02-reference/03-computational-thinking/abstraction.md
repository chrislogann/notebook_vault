# Abstraction

## Overview
> Abstraction is the process of filtering out irrelevant details and specific characteristics to concentrate strictly on the universal traits necessary to solve a problem.

## Core Concepts
- **Distinguishing Characteristics:** Effective abstraction separates general, common elements (the pattern) from non-essential, individual variations (the specifics).
- **The Mechanics of Filtering:** By actively removing irrelevant characteristics and specific details, you isolate the core mechanics of a system.
- **Creating a Model:** The ultimate goal of abstraction is to produce a "model"—a highly accurate, general idea of what the problem is and how to solve it without being distracted by specific instances.
