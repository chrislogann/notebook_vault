# Anthropic

## Overview
> A leading research company focused specifically on developing safe, reliable, and interpretable large language models (LLMs). Anthropic's core philosophy centers on Constitutional AI, ensuring models adhere to a defined set of ethical guidelines rather than just basic safety filters.

## Core Concepts
- **Constitutional AI:** A method where LLM alignment is achieved by training the model against an explicit constitution or set of principles, minimizing reliance solely on human feedback (RLHF).
- **Safety Research:** Emphasis on interpretability and robustness, focusing on preventing misuse and unintended behaviors in powerful AI systems.
- **Anthropic Claude Models:** The flagship suite of models developed by the company, known for their long context windows and adherence to ethical guardrails specified during training.
