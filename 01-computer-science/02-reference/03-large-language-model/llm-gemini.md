# Gemini

## Overview
> Gemini is a family of multimodal AI models designed by Google DeepMind, notable for its native ability to process and reason across various types of information—including text, images, audio, and video—simultaneously and cohesively. Unlike models trained sequentially, Gemini was built from the ground up to understand multiple data modalities at the same level of complexity.

## Core Concepts
- **Native Multimodality:** The capacity to treat different inputs (text, image pixels, sound waves) not as separate streams requiring specialized processing, but as interconnected components contributing to a single understanding.
- **Scale and Optimization:** Gemini exists in various sizes (e.g., Ultra, Pro, Nano), allowing it to be deployed efficiently across disparate computational environments—from massive data centers running complex reasoning tasks down to on-device mobile applications.
- **Advanced Reasoning:** The architecture facilitates sophisticated task completion, enabling the model to understand intricate relationships between different data types and perform complex logical deductions that require cross-modal synthesis.
