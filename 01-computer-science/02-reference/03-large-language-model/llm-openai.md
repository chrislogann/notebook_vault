# Openai

## Overview
> OpenAI is a leading research organization and commercial developer specializing in cutting-edge artificial intelligence. Its primary focus is advancing large language models (LLMs) and generative AI capabilities to solve complex problems across various industries while ensuring responsible deployment.

## Core Concepts
- **Transformer Architecture:** The foundational technological basis for modern LLMs, utilizing self-attention mechanisms that allow the model to weigh the importance of different words in the input sequence relative to each other, regardless of distance.
- **Scaling Laws and Emergent Abilities:** Demonstrates that performance gains in AI models are often predictable based on increasing scale (parameters, data volume). Beyond certain scale thresholds, capabilities like complex reasoning or multi-step planning can "emerge" seemingly suddenly.
- **Prompt Engineering:** The practice of carefully designing and refining inputs (prompts) to guide the LLM toward desired outputs, acting as a form of input structuring that dictates task execution without retraining the model weights.
