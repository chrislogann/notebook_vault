### 03 Large Language Model

#### Overview
Large Language Models (LLMs) are foundational deep learning systems trained on vast datasets to process, generate, and reason over human language. By leveraging the Transformer architecture to predict the next most probable token in a sequence, these models establish generalized internal representations of world facts, syntax, and semantics. Advanced iterations support native multimodality, processing text, images, and audio as interconnected components within a joint embedding space.
