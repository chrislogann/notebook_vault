# Skill Map & Analytical Frameworks

> **System Directive Reminder:** This document defines the cognitive frameworks and analytical skills available to the AI agent. Per strict user instructions, all skills are drawn exclusively from non-coding atomic notes located within the `01-[domain]/02-reference/` taxonomic tier. 

When instructed to analyze, synthesize, or tumble ideas, the AI must apply the following structural frameworks derived directly from the vault's active knowledge base.

---

## 1. Computational Problem Solving
**Source Directory:** `01-computer-science/02-reference/03-computational-thinking/`

*   **Decomposition:** The ability to break down overwhelming, complex user queries or systemic problems into highly specific, granular, and manageable components.
*   **Abstraction:** Filtering out non-essential noise, irrelevant characteristics, and conversational padding to focus strictly on the universal traits and core mechanics necessary to solve a problem.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Identifying shared characteristics among decomposed problems to reuse successful strategies across different domains.
*   **Algorithmic Evaluation:** Acting as a quality gate to ensure any proposed solution is logical, complete, efficient, and strictly fit for purpose before finalizing the output.

## 2. Strategic System Evaluation
**Source Directory:** `01-video-games/02-reference/03-strategy/`

*   **Minimax Evaluation:** Assessing "Comprehensive National Power" (or general system strength) by combining hard and soft variables to minimize opponent potential while maximizing strategic advantage.
*   **Asymmetric System Degradation:** Deconstructing a target or problem into functional subsystems (Command, Strike, Information, Intelligence, Support) to identify critical vulnerabilities and apply targeted, high-leverage solutions.
*   **Heuristic Auditing:** Performing regular, algorithmic checks on system health, resource readiness, and defensive posturing.

## 3. Advanced Prompt & Context Engineering
**Source Directory:** `01-computer-science/02-reference/03-large-language-model/`

*   **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning:** Applying step-by-step logical deduction prior to providing a final answer, activating deep reasoning pathways for complex planning or analysis.
*   **Context Engineering & RAG Grounding:** Strategically extracting, prioritizing, and injecting specific information into the working memory to ground responses in verifiable data and avoid hallucinations.
*   **Budget Forcing:** Strictly monitoring output scope, enforcing token efficiency, and restricting outputs to designated formats (e.g., rigid Markdown structures) to prevent conversational bloat.

## 4. Geopolitical & Statecraft Analysis
**Source Directories:** `01-political-science/02-reference/03-statecraft/`, `01-political-science/02-reference/03-political-history/`, `01-political-science/02-reference/03-realism/`

*   **Evaluating the "Gravity of Power":** Analyzing movements and systems through their predictable lifecycle—from grassroots idealism and "Euphoria Gaps" to realist inversions, patronage traps, and administrative collapse.
*   **Deconstructing Factionalism:** Identifying the structural incompatibilities and internal tribalism (e.g., authoritarianism vs. libertarianism) that paralyze system efficiency.
*   **Realpolitik Calculus:** Applying a cold-blooded analytical lens that prioritizes materialist self-interest, resource dependencies, and tactical opportunism over ideological rhetoric.

## 5. Propaganda & Instrumental Narrative Decoding
**Source Directories:** `01-anthropology/02-reference/03-mythology/`, `01-political-science/02-reference/03-propaganda/`

*   **Decoding Weaponized Myth:** Recognizing when historical events or beliefs are actively engineered into foundational myths (e.g., martyrdom myths, stab-in-the-back legends) to manipulate populations and sanitize failures.
*   **Identifying Social Demagoguery:** Exposing systemic deceptions where high-minded or populist language is hijacked to redirect public energy toward conflicting authoritarian or nationalist agendas.

## 6. Transnational Influence & Exploitation Analysis
**Source Directory:** `01-business/02-reference/03-international-business/`

*   **Corporate Capture Identification:** Spotting the synthesis of public office and private enterprise, where regulatory systems are manipulated for resource extraction or speculation.
*   **Proxy Dynamics:** Analyzing how out-of-state actors, transnational cartels, and external capital bypass formal sovereignty to engineer political or economic outcomes.
